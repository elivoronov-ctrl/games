# Underpaint

A quiet kid with a backpack of spray cans sneaks through the night subway, painting a gray city back into color, one station at a time.

**Play:** https://games.getsimpleinvoice.app/underpaint/

## Story (6 chapters + epilogue)
1. **First Color**: Canal Street Station, three spots, one guard.
2. **The Yard**: Northline Train Yard; live tracks with trains.
3. **Rush Hour Ghosts**: Market Square's kiosk maze, more guards.
4. **Glass Eyes**: sweeping security cameras.
5. **The Express**: three nonstop lines in the B4 tunnels.
6. **Central Hall**: four guards, three cameras, two lines, eight walls.
7. **Epilogue**: Morning Commute.

Paint every tag spot, then reach the EXIT. Guard flashlights and camera beams fill the eye meter. When it's full you're busted and lose nerve. Trains flash red before roaring through. Bonuses for speed and for never being spotted (Ghost).

## Controls
| Action | Keyboard | Mouse / Touch |
|---|---|---|
| Move | WASD / Arrows | Hold the mouse where you want to go / on-screen stick |
| Paint | Space (or stand still on a spot) | PAINT button |
| Pause / Mute | P or Esc / M | ⏸ / ♪ buttons |

Single player. Canvas 2D + Web Audio, all procedural.
