The city painted its subway gray. One quiet kid with a backpack of spray cans is going to paint it back.

Move: WASD / Arrows, the on-screen stick, or hold the mouse where you want to go.
Paint: stand on a glowing tag spot and hold Space / PAINT (or just stand still). Paint every spot, then slip out of the EXIT.
Stay unseen: guards' flashlights and camera beams fill the eye meter. When it's full you're busted. Trains on the tracks flash red before they roar through, so get off the rails.
