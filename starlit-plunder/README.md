# Starlit Plunder

A space-pirate saga in six chapters. The Crown Armada stole Captain Juniper Vex's crew: plunder convoys, rebuild a star-map, storm a prison fort and face the Iron Admiral's dreadnought.

**Play:** https://games.getsimpleinvoice.app/starlit-plunder/

## Story chapters (each opens with a cutscene)
1. **The Empty Cove**: plunder three Crown cargo barges
2. **The Rock Garden**: shoot glowing asteroids to recover 5 star-map fragments, dodging drifting mines
3. **Convoy of the Crown**: sink the treasure galleon and its broadsides
4. **Fort Halcyon**: destroy 4 shield generators, then scoop up 6 crew pods
5. **The Veil Nebula**: fight 15 hunters with almost no visibility
6. **The Iron Admiral**: break the Dreadnought Sovereign's turrets, then its core
Then an epilogue.

Pickups: gold (score), ✚ hull repair, ★ cannon upgrades (up to a 5-way spread), ◈ map fragments, ☺ crew pods. Three bombs per chapter clear bullets and blast everything nearby.
