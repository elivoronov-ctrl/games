GLYPHBOUND

Deep in the green, a temple wakes. Its guardians speak only in glyphs.

Guide the explorer Ixa through six story chapters of the Temple of the Seventh Sun. Every guardian is a card duel.
Each turn you get 3 sun energy and draw 5 glyph cards. Attack cards deal damage, Ward cards give block that soaks hits this turn, Skill cards burn, poison, heal or draw. Watch the guardian's intent above its head and plan around it. Win to add a new glyph to your deck.
Play a card: click / tap it, or keys 1–7   End turn: E / Enter / button
Story: Space / tap to continue   Pause: P / Esc   Mute: M
