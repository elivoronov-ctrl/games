# Glyphbound

A story-driven deck-building card game in a jungle temple: guide the explorer Ixa through six chapters, beating each glyph guardian in a card duel.

**Play:** https://games.getsimpleinvoice.app/glyphbound/

## Story chapters
1. **The Overgrown Gate**: the Vine Serpent
2. **The Hall of Whispers**: the Stone Monkey
3. **The Flooded Sanctum**: the Jade Crocodile
4. **The Bridge of Bats**: the Echo Bat Swarm
5. **The Sun Forge**: the Obsidian Jaguar
6. **The Heart of the Temple**: the Masked Sun King (final boss)
7. Epilogue: Rain

Each chapter opens with a short story scene. Every turn you get 3 sun energy and draw 5 cards: attacks, wards (block), and skills like burn, poison, weaken, strength, healing and drawing more cards. The guardian shows its next move above its head. Win a duel to add 1 of 3 new glyph cards to your deck (17 card types in all). If you lose, retry the chapter with the deck you started it with. Progress and best score are saved on your device.

## Controls
| Action | Keyboard | Mouse / Touch |
|---|---|---|
| Play card | 1–7 | Click / tap the card |
| End turn | E / Enter | End turn button |
| Story | Space / Enter | Tap / Continue |
| Pick reward | 1 / 2 / 3 | Click / tap |
| Pause / Mute | P or Esc / M | ⏸ / ♪ |

Single player. Canvas 2D scenes, DOM cards, Web Audio synth.
