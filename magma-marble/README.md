# Magma Marble

Roll a basalt rescue orb across floating volcanic rock, scoop up stranded villagers, and reach the airship before Cinder Isle erupts.

**Play:** https://games.getsimpleinvoice.app/magma-marble/

## 10 levels
1. **Ashfield Village**: gentle intro, gems and three villagers.
2. **Obsidian Steps**: ember bombs start falling (red warning rings).
3. **Sulfur Springs**: crumbling rock tiles.
4. **Ember Bridges**: moving slabs over the lava.
5. **Crumbling Caldera**: longer paths, villagers on crumbling ledges.
6. **Geyser Gardens**: steam geysers launch you and add heat.
7. **Magma Falls**: everything at once, faster bombs.
8. **Pumice Peaks**: tall climbs and wide gaps.
9. **Smoke Tunnel**: low visibility, thick fog.
10. **The Crater Heart**: the longest, hardest course; seven villagers.

Each level has an eruption timer; rescue every villager, then touch the glowing airship pad. Heat builds from bombs and geysers, and too much costs a life. Clearing a level grants +1 life and time/level bonuses.

## Controls
| Action | Keyboard | Mouse / Touch |
|---|---|---|
| Roll | WASD / Arrows | On-screen stick (drag) |
| Jump | Space | JUMP button / click |
| Pause / Mute | P or Esc / M | ⏸ / ♪ buttons |

Single player. Three.js (WebGL) + Web Audio, all procedural, no external assets.
