# Rooftop Routes

Draw the flight path for your homing pigeon across the city rooftops, then hold FLAP to time your way past hawks, chimney smoke, searchlights, swinging cranes and lightning on the way home to the loft.

**Play:** https://games.getsimpleinvoice.app/rooftop-routes/

## 10 levels
1. **Bakery Row**: learn to draw routes around water towers and pick up seeds.
2. **Laundry Lines**: clotheslines block your route, so thread the gaps.
3. **Chimney Town**: chimneys puff smoke in a rhythm (an orange ring warns before each puff).
4. **Pigeon Plaza**: race day, with rival pigeons and a giant statue in the middle.
5. **Hawk Heights**: a hawk circles the tall roofs.
6. **Windy Spires**: gusts push you off course toward the spires, and a hawk patrols the skyline.
7. **Neon Night**: dark streets and sweeping searchlights.
8. **Crane Yard**: rotating tower-crane arms.
9. **Thunderstorm**: lightning strikes where the rings glow, plus wind and a hawk.
10. **Grand Derby**: every hazard at once on the longest route.

Every level has a par time: under par wins gold, under 1.3x par silver, and any safe landing bronze. Seeds, leftover feathers and speed add to your score. Medals, unlocked levels and your best total are saved locally.

## Controls
| Action | Keyboard | Mouse / Touch |
|---|---|---|
| Draw route | Arrows / WASD steer the pen | Drag from the pigeon (or from the route's end to extend it) |
| Undo / Clear | Backspace or Z / C | ↶ / ✕ |
| Launch | Enter | FLY! |
| Flap (speed boost, uses stamina) | Hold Space | Hold FLAP or hold anywhere |
| Pause / Mute | P or Esc / M | ⏸ / ♪ |

Single player. Canvas 2D + Web Audio, all procedural. On a phone in portrait the city map rotates to fill the screen.
