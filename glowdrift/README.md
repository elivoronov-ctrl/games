# Glowdrift

You are a tiny bioluminescent jellyfish. Pulse your way down through ten reefs, each darker than the last, and keep your fading glow alive until you get past the Leviathan.

**Play:** https://games.getsimpleinvoice.app/glowdrift/

## How it works
- You move by **pulsing**. Each pulse pushes you the way your bell is facing. Water drag slows you down and you always sink a little.
- **Your glow is your life and your light.** It slowly drains. Eat plankton sparks to refill it, and avoid hazards, which cost glow. If it runs out, you can retry the reef or start over from reef 1.
- Reach the **anemone gate** to clear a reef. Each reef has a **gold star** for collecting all **3 pearls** and another for beating the **par time** (up to 3 stars per reef, 30 in total). Your score also gets bonuses for time and leftover glow.

## The 10 reefs
1. **Sunlit Shallows**: tutorial reef with bright water and light shafts.
2. **Kelp Maze**: kelp forests that slow you down, and walls to weave around.
3. **Current Canyon**: strong currents to ride and to cross.
4. **Urchin Gardens**: a floor covered in spiny urchins, plus urchins that drift.
5. **Anglerfish Dark**: anglerfish with glowing lures. They chase anything that glows.
6. **Vent Fields**: hydrothermal vents that erupt in bursts. They throw you upward and burn your glow.
7. **Shipwreck**: narrow wooden corridors with rusted anchor chains swinging through them.
8. **Eel Tunnels**: moray eels dart out of holes in the walls. Their glowing eyes warn you first.
9. **Abyss Trench**: a vertical dive in near-total darkness, where your glow drains faster.
10. **Leviathan's Lair**: a final chase with a giant pursuer. Swim right and do not stop.

Reefs unlock as you clear them and can be replayed from the title screen. The best score, stars and unlocked reefs are saved locally.

## Notes
Single player. Canvas 2D and a Web Audio synth (ambient drone, pentatonic chimes and sound effects). All graphics are procedural: verlet tentacles, a light-mask darkness system, parallax silhouettes and particles. Works on desktop and on iPhone/iPad in both portrait and landscape.
