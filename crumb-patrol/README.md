# Crumb Patrol

Drive DOT-9, a small robot vacuum with big dreams, through six filthy rooms: suck up crumbs, dodge the cat and upgrade your bot between shifts.

**Play:** https://games.getsimpleinvoice.app/crumb-patrol/

3D (Three.js r160) · single player · progression · procedural graphics and Web Audio synth sound.

## How it works
Each shift is a room with a cleanliness target. Clean up to the target, then roll back onto the green charging dock to finish. The floor visibly wipes clean under you as you drive. Your battery drains all the time, faster with turbo or in puddles, so plan trips back to the dock. If the battery hits zero, DOT-9 powers down (game over for that shift), but you keep the dust credits you collected.

## Progression
- **6 shifts:** Kitchen → Living Room → Kids' Room → Garage → Laundry & Bath → Party Hall
- **Dust credits** buy upgrades in the Dock Workshop, 5 tiers each: Cyclone Suction (radius), Battery Pack (+15 s), Motor (speed), Turbo Brush (longer turbo, shorter cooldown), Bumper Foam (shorter stuns)
- **Model ranks** Mk I to Mk VI come from total dirt collected, each with a small passive boost
- Shift bonuses for leftover battery and "Spotless" (95%+) rooms. Progress is saved, so you can continue later.

| Shift | New twist |
|---|---|
| Kitchen | Tutorial: crumbs, dust bunnies, battery and dock |
| Living Room | Whiskers the cat crouches and pounces. Socks jam your brush |
| Kids' Room | Runaway RC car, Lego bricks, glitter |
| Garage | Slippery oil spills, crumb-stealing mice |
| Laundry & Bath | Battery-draining puddles, Biscuit the muddy dog leaves new dirt |
| Party Hall | Huge confetti-covered room with the whole gang |

## Controls
| Action | Keyboard | Mouse / touch |
|---|---|---|
| Drive | WASD / arrow keys | Hold left mouse button (drive toward cursor) · on-screen joystick |
| Turbo (plows through pets) | Space / Shift | Right-click · TURBO button |
| Pause | P / Esc | ⏸ button |
| Mute | M | ♪ button |

Single player. Works on desktop and iPhone/iPad (portrait or landscape). The high score is saved in localStorage.
