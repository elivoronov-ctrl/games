# Midnight Plow

Drive a snowplow through a sleeping city and scrape ten districts clear before dawn.

**Play:** https://games.getsimpleinvoice.app/midnight-plow/

## How it works
- Each district is a grid of snowed-in streets. Push snow aside with your blade and reach the **target %** before the dawn clock hits zero.
- Hitting walls, parked cars or night traffic costs seconds. Grab glowing **☕ thermos** cups for +10 seconds.
- **Black ice** (blue patches) makes the plow slide. In whiteouts and blizzards, **snow falls back** onto streets you already cleared.
- The minimap shows clean streets (dark), snow (white), parked cars (red), traffic (blue) and thermos cups (orange).
- Up to 3 stars per district: clear the target, reach 90%, and finish with 20+ seconds left. Districts unlock as you clear them. Best score and stars are saved locally.

## The 10 districts
1. **Quiet Lane**: a small tutorial district.
2. **Parked Up**: cars buried along the curbs.
3. **Market Square**: an open plaza with a holiday tree.
4. **Black Ice**: slippery patches everywhere.
5. **Night Buses**: moving traffic on the main roads.
6. **Whiteout**: thick fog, with snow refilling the streets.
7. **Ambulance Run**: heavy traffic, ice and lots of parked cars.
8. **Canal Bridges**: wide ice sheets and traffic.
9. **Blizzard**: a huge district with heavy refill, fog and traffic.
10. **Midnight Storm**: everything at once, in the biggest city.

## Controls
| Action | Keyboard | Touch / Mouse |
|---|---|---|
| Gas / reverse | W / S or ↑ / ↓ | GAS / REV buttons |
| Steer | A / D or ← / → | ◀ ▶ buttons |
| Horn | H | – |
| Pause | P / Esc | ⏸ |
| Mute | M | ♪ |
| Next / retry | Enter | Buttons on the card |

Single player. Three.js (procedural city, instanced snow cells, headlight spotlight, snowfall particles) and a Web Audio synth (engine drone, scraping noise, bell music). Works on desktop and iPhone/iPad.
