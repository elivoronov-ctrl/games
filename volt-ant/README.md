# Volt Ant

Pip, a tiny ant who is immune to electricity, carries the last spark of power across a dying motherboard to restart the Elder Machine.

**Play:** https://games.getsimpleinvoice.app/volt-ant/

## Story (6 chapters + epilogue)
1. **Cold Boot**: the spark falls onto Pip's back. Cross the Motherboard Plains and learn to climb walls.
2. **Capacitor Canyon**: leaking capacitors arc blue lightning on a rhythm.
3. **The Fan Winds**: cooling fans throw you over wide gaps.
4. **RAM Valley**: ride shuffling memory blocks over pits of exposed pins.
5. **The Dust Plague**: dust bunnies and glitch bugs. Stomp them.
6. **The Static Wave**: a wave of static erases the board behind you. Run!
- **Epilogue: Hello, Pip.**

Each chapter opens with a story card. Collect binary **bits**, find the **3 golden chips**, touch green **LEDs** to save, and deliver the spark to the **CPU socket**. Chapters unlock as you clear them. Best score and chips are saved locally.

## Controls
| Action | Keyboard | Touch | Mouse |
|---|---|---|---|
| Run | ← / → or A / D | ◀ ▶ | Hold on either side of Pip |
| Jump / wall-jump | Space / W / ↑ | JUMP | Click above Pip |
| Climb a wall | Push into it | Push into it | Hold toward it |
| Slide down a wall | ↓ / S | ▼ | – |
| Pause | P / Esc | ⏸ | ⏸ |
| Mute | M | ♪ | ♪ |

Single player. Canvas 2D with procedural, seeded levels and a Web Audio chiptune synth. Works on desktop and iPhone/iPad (portrait and landscape).
