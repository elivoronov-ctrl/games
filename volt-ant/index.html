VOLT ANT

A tiny ant, a dying computer and one last spark.

Run with ←/→ or A/D, jump with Space / W / ↑. Ants grip anything: push into a wall to climb it, press ↓ to slide down, and jump off walls to kick away.
Collect bits and the 3 golden chips hidden in each chapter, touch green LEDs to save, and carry the spark to the socket at the end.
Watch out for exposed pins, arcing capacitors, fan gusts, dust bunnies, glitch bugs and the Static Wave. Stomp on enemies to pop them.
Mouse: hold on either side of the ant to run and click above it to jump. Touch: ◀ ▶ ▼ JUMP.
