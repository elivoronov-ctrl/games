Orrery Owl

The stars have fallen out of tune. Wind them back, one night at a time.

How to play: Tock the clockwork owl keeps the observatory's orrery in time. Star-notes ride along 3 orbit rings toward the brass strike arm at the top. Strike a ring's key the moment its note reaches the arm.
• Inner D/← · Middle F/↓ · Outer J/→  or tap/click a ring or the big ring buttons.
• Long arcs: hold until the tail passes. Eclipses: two dark notes at once, strike both.
• Misses loosen the spring. If it goes slack, the night is lost. P/Esc pause · M mute.
