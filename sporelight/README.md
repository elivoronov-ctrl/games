# Sporelight

Tap your glowing cap to relight a sleeping forest, grow a mushroom colony, and keep the Gloom Moths away for six story chapters.

**Play:** https://games.getsimpleinvoice.app/sporelight/

## Story (6 chapters + epilogue)
1. **A Single Glow**: Mossy, the last glowing mushroom, meets Old Shelf.
2. **The Gloom Moths**: moths start stealing light; the Gloom meter appears.
3. **Whispering Roots**: the mycelium speaks; golden spores start drifting by.
4. **The Toad Ferryman**: lanterns cross the Black Pond; armoured moths arrive.
5. **Moonfall**: a sliver of moon lands; moon toadstools sprout.
6. **The Long Dark**: the Gloom Mother attacks; gather 2.5M spores and bloom the Heartcap.
7. **Epilogue**: Morning in Hollowmere.

Six growers (Spore Puffs, Glow Gnats, Mycelium Threads, Lantern Caps, Moon Toadstools, Elder Shelf), upgrades at 10/25/50 owned, four tap upgrades, golden spores (harvest or Frenzy). Progress autosaves (Continue on the title screen).

## Controls
| Action | Keyboard | Mouse / Touch |
|---|---|---|
| Shed spores | Space / Enter | Tap the big mushroom |
| Pop moths | Space (near the cap) | Tap a moth |
| Buy grower | 1 – 6 | Tap a row in the shop |
| Buy upgrade | U | Tap a ★ button |
| Bloom the Heartcap | B | Tap the Bloom button |
| Pause / Mute | P or Esc / M | ⏸ / ♪ buttons |

Lose if the Gloom meter reaches 100%. Single player. Canvas 2D + Web Audio, no external assets.
