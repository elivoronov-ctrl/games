# Twister Trail

Chase ten tornadoes across the plains in a storm-chaser truck, dropping sensor probes right where the funnel will pass.

**Play:** https://games.getsimpleinvoice.app/twister-trail/

## 10 storms (levels)
1. **Wheat Belt**: a slow rope tornado over golden fields.
2. **Sunflower Rows**: faster funnel that zig-zags.
3. **Red Badlands**: more flying debris.
4. **Hail Alley**: dodge white hail rings.
5. **Night Watch**: pitch dark, headlights and radar only.
6. **Lightning Mesa**: yellow rings mean a bolt is coming.
7. **Twin Funnels**: two tornadoes at once.
8. **Flooded Delta**: water slows the truck to a crawl.
9. **Dust Devil Desert**: a wide wedge full of debris.
10. **The Supercell**: twin wedges, hail, lightning and night together.

Each storm needs a set number of probes swallowed by the funnel before it dies out. Probes run on batteries, so read the tornado's track on the radar and get ahead of it. Staying close films storm footage for bonus points. Inflow winds pull you in, and the core wrecks your truck. The tornado also tears up houses, trees and hay bales along its path.

## Controls
| Action | Keyboard | Touch |
|---|---|---|
| Drive / steer | WASD / Arrows | Left stick |
| Drop probe | Space | PROBE button |
| Turbo | Shift | TURBO button |
| Pause / Mute | P or Esc / M | ⏸ / ♪ buttons |

Single player. Three.js (3D) + Web Audio, all procedural. Progress and high score are saved locally.
