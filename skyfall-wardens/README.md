# Skyfall Wardens

A meteor shower is falling on the moon colony's greenhouse domes. Drive your flak rover, blast meteors out of the sky, catch the stardust they leave behind, and level up while other wardens share your sky.

**Play:** https://games.getsimpleinvoice.app/skyfall-wardens/

## Progression
- **6 showers**: Perseid Drizzle, Iron Rain (armored 2-hit meteors), Splitter Storm (they burst into shards), Comet Night (fast comets), Crystal Cascade (golden crystal meteors full of stardust), and The Great Shower, where the giant **Old Ironface** must be destroyed before it lands.
- **XP levels**: stardust is XP. Each level-up earns 1 of 3 upgrades between showers (Wide Flak, Quick Loader, Rail Shells, Twin Barrels, Turbo Wheels, Dust Magnet, Dome Glazier, Chain Burst), up to 5 ranks each.
- **Rover colors** unlock at warden levels 3, 5, 8 and 11, plus a white rover for saving the colony (saved locally with your best score).
- Each of the 5 domes takes 3 hits. Lose all five and the colony falls.

## Controls
| Action | Keyboard | Mouse / Touch |
|---|---|---|
| Aim + fire flak | Arrows to aim, Space to fire | Click / tap the sky (hold to keep firing) |
| Drive rover (catch stardust) | A / D | ◀ ▶ buttons |
| Pick upgrade | 1 / 2 / 3 | Tap a card |
| Pause / Mute | P or Esc / M | ⏸ / ♪ |

## Multiplayer
Type a name and you join the shared sky automatically (Supabase Realtime broadcast + presence, no accounts or database). Other wardens' rovers, reticles, shells and flak bursts appear live on your moon, with an online count and a score leaderboard. When another warden clears a shower, everyone online gets a 15-second **Rally** (stardust x2), and you hear about it when someone loses a dome. If the connection fails, the game plays solo.

Canvas 2D + Web Audio, all procedural.
