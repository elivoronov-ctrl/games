# Skystring Derby

Fly a festival kite through ten 3D courses: thread the ribbon gates, ride the gusts, dodge birds, storms and fireworks, and beat the legendary Dragon Kite.

**Play:** https://games.getsimpleinvoice.app/skystring-derby/

## 10 courses
1. Meadow Morning: learn the ribbons
2. Rice Terraces: swallows
3. Harbor Breeze: hot-air balloons over the water
4. Bamboo Valley: tall stalks, twisty canyon
5. Lantern Dusk: rising sky lanterns
6. Thunderhead: storm clouds that flash
7. Mountain Pass: granite spires
8. Desert Thermals: updrafts, vultures
9. Night Fireworks: bursting shells
10. The Dragon Kite: beat the Dragon to the line

Each course is faster, longer and tighter, with smaller gates and more hazards. Gates add time, perfect passes and combos add score, and gust orbs refill your boost. Three hits snap your string.

## Controls
| Action | Keyboard | Mouse | Touch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Steer | WASD / Arrows | Move the mouse | Joystick |
| Gust boost | Space / Shift | Hold the button | GUST button |
| Pause / Mute | P or Esc / M | ⏸ / ♪ | ⏸ / ♪ |

Single player. Three.js 0.160 + Web Audio, all procedural.
