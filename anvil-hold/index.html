ANVIL HOLD

Deep under Mount Grimmel, the clan's Great Anvil is the heart of every forge. Cinder imps, rock trolls and lava slimes are crawling up the tunnels to snuff it out. Build defences, hold the line, and grow your forge between battles.

Build: pick a tower (1-4 or the bar), then click/tap an empty rock tile. Enemies march along the glowing path.
🔨 Hammer all-rounder · 🔥 Bellows burns everything nearby (not bats) · 🎯 Ballista long range, bolts pierce · ❄️ Frost rune slows.
Click a tower to upgrade (U) or sell (S). Space/⚔️ calls the next wave early for bonus gold, F/⏩ speeds up. Arrow keys + Enter also place towers.
Each Deep has 8 waves with a boss at the end. Earn XP, level up and spend forge points on permanent upgrades.
Online: other dwarves in the hold show up in the clan list, their hammers appear on your map when you defend the same Deep, and every wave anyone clears sends tribute gold to everyone.
