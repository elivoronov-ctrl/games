LANTERN HOLLOW

Catch fireflies, light the Great Lamp, and keep the long night away, together with lantern-bearers from around the world.

Catch fireflies by walking into them. They fill your lantern 🏮.
Bank them at the Great Lamp in the middle of the hollow to earn glow and XP. Each level-up lets you pick an upgrade.
Every night the Lamp needs enough glow, or the dark wins. Five nights until dawn.
Watch out: moths steal from full lanterns, frogs lash out from ponds, wind blows and owls swoop. Dash through danger!
Banking near another player gives a +25% together bonus.
Keys: WASD/Arrows move · Space dash · P/Esc pause · M mute · Mouse: hold to walk toward the cursor · Touch: stick + DASH.
