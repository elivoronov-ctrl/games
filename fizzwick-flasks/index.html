FIZZWICK FLASKS

Old Master Fizzwick has left you, his newest apprentice, in charge of the lab. Swap glowing reagents, fill the cauldron and brew every potion order before the hourglass runs dry.

Swap two neighbouring reagents to line up 3+ of a kind. Drag, or tap one then its neighbour · Keyboard: arrows/WASD move, Space/Enter picks, then an arrow swaps.
Orders: clear the reagents a potion needs to brew it (+time, +XP). Brew enough potions to clear the workshop.
Specials: 4 in a row = Fizz Bomb (clears a row/column) · L or T shape = Cross Blast · 5 in a row = Philosopher's Stone (swap it to clear a whole type, and it sends a fizz gift to everyone online).
Hazards: sealed flasks take 2 hits, chains lock tiles, sludge spreads. 6 workshops, ending with the Grand Elixir.
