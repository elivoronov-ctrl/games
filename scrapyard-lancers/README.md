# Scrapyard Lancers

A scrappy little scavenger robot enters the roughest jousting tournament in the junkyard: charge with your lance, knock rivals off a floating scrap ring, and win the Golden Gasket.

**Play:** https://games.getsimpleinvoice.app/scrapyard-lancers/

## Story (6 chapters + epilogue)
1. **Rust & Glory**: Rivet's first bout against Pail, with Grandpa Gears teaching the ropes.
2. **Twin Trouble**: practice drones, then the Toaster Twins two-on-one, on a ring with slippery oil slicks.
3. **Magnetic Personality**: Magna controls a sweeping crane magnet that drags every robot around.
4. **The Compactor**: a drone gauntlet, then heavyweight Crusher Kade on a ring that shrinks every second.
5. **Live Wires**: Sparkjaw and his drone, plus spark coils that flash yellow and then zap anything standing on them.
6. **The Baron of Bolts**: the final boss, with magnet, coils and oil all at once. He calls in drones at 2/3 and 1/3 balance.

Between chapters you visit the **Workshop** and bolt on 1 of 3 parts (Thick Plating, Long Lance, Big Boiler, Spiked Treads, Tungsten Tip, Kite Shield, Spare Spark, Gyro Anchor).

## How to win a bout
Hit rivals with the outer half of your lance while moving fast. Charging hits land hardest. A hit drains their balance and knocks them back, so you win by toppling them (balance at zero) or by pushing them off the ring. A braced shield blocks lances from the front and bounces the attacker back. Golden bolts give points and refill steam. You have 3 sparks (lives) per chapter.

## Controls
| Action | Keyboard | Touch |
|---|---|---|
| Move | WASD / Arrows | Left stick |
| Charge (costs steam) | Space / J | CHARGE |
| Brace shield (hold) | Shift / K | BRACE |
| Advance story / skip | Enter or Space / Esc | Tap the dialog box |
| Pick a part | 1 / 2 / 3 | Tap a card |
| Pause / Mute | P or Esc / M | ⏸ / ♪ |

Single player. Three.js (WebGL) + Web Audio, all procedural. High score and furthest chapter are saved locally.
