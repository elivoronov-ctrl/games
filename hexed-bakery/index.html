Hexed Bakery

The ovens are cursed. The croissants are haunted. Dawn is six hours away.

Survive the night shift from midnight to 6 AM while haunted pastries swarm the bakery.
Your baker throws rolling pins automatically at the nearest ghost. Grab the glowing soul crumbs they drop to level up and pick 1 of 3 upgrades.
Move: WASD / Arrows, or drag the joystick   Dash: Space / Shift / DASH   Pause: P / Esc   Mute: M
Online: other bakers on the same night shift appear in your kitchen. When anyone levels up, everybody gets a warm loaf (+HP).
