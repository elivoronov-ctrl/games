# Liana Leap

Swing vine to vine as Mossy the sloth across ten stretches of rainforest canopy to catch up with the family at the Great Kapok.

**Play:** https://games.getsimpleinvoice.app/liana-leap/

3D (Three.js) · single player · 10 levels · pendulum-physics vine swinging

## The 10 stretches
1. **Morning Mist**: gentle vines, learn to hang and fly
2. **Fig Grove**: wider gaps, lots of figs
3. **Breezy Heights**: wind gusts push you around
4. **Snake Branches**: tree snakes slither up and down some vines
5. **Monsoon**: rain, lightning and slippery vines (grip meter)
6. **Thorn Tangle**: bobbing thorn pods between vines
7. **Toucan Flyway**: toucans zoom across the canopy
8. **Waterfall Gorge**: falling water drags you down
9. **Firefly Night**: darkness, fireflies mark the vines
10. **Storm Chase**: a monster storm front chases you to the Great Kapok

Fruit (+100), golden orchids (+500, 3 per stretch), big-air leaps with combos, time and heart bonuses. Unlocked stretches and best score are saved.

## Controls
| Action | Keyboard | Mouse / touch |
|---|---|---|
| Grab vine (hold) / let go to fly | Space (or J/Z) | Hold mouse button / hold GRAB or touch the screen |
| Pump the swing | ← → or A D | ◀ ▶ buttons |
| Climb up / down the vine | ↑ ↓ or W S | ▲ ▼ buttons |
| Pause | P / Esc | ⏸ |
| Mute | M | ♪ |

Debug hook: `window.__sloth` (`state`, `level`, `player`, `goto(n)`, `toAnchor(i)`, `teleport(x,y)`, `win()`).
