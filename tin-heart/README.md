# Tin Heart

A 3D roguelite through a toy factory gone wrong: one wind-up tin soldier, ten factory floors, and a random part to pick after every floor.

**Play:** https://games.getsimpleinvoice.app/tin-heart/

## The 10 floors
1. Wrapping Room: marching drummer bots
2. Spring Press: spinning tops and sweeping blades
3. Paint Shop: RC cars and fire vents
4. Conveyor Hall: belts that carry you and the toys
5. Jack-in-the-Box Pit: boss **Jack Colossus**
6. Marble Run: giant bouncing marbles
7. Teddy Stitchery: tanky teddies, belts and blades
8. Furnace Forge: six fire vents
9. Gear Tower: blades, marbles and big mixed waves
10. Music Box Throne: final boss **The Music Box Queen** (spiral bullet patterns)

Every floor adds enemies, speed and hazards. Clear the waves, step into the golden gear, then pick **1 of 3 random parts**: Brass Corks, Rapid Ratchet, Twin Barrel, Bouncy Corks, Tight Spring, Spare Tin Heart, Brass Plating, Magnet Key, Oiled Joints, Needle Corks. Lose all your hearts and the run starts again from floor 1. Best score and deepest floor are saved.

| Action | Keyboard | Mouse | Touch |

| Aim | auto-aim at nearest toy | Mouse | auto-aim |
| Fire | Hold J | Hold left button | Hold FIRE |
| Dash | Space / Shift | Right click | DASH |
| Pick part | 1 / 2 / 3 | Click | Tap |
| Pause / Mute | P or Esc / M | ⏸ / ♪ | ⏸ / ♪ |

Single player. Three.js 0.160 and a Web Audio synth, with all graphics built from primitives.
