TIN HEART

The toy factory has wound itself up wrong. One little tin soldier still ticks true.

Fight down 10 factory floors, from the Wrapping Room to the Music Box Throne. Clear every hostile toy, then step into the golden gear to descend. Between floors pick 1 of 3 random parts. Fall apart and you start again from floor 1.
Move: WASD / Arrows / joystick   Aim: mouse (auto-aim on keys & touch)
Fire: hold mouse / J / FIRE   Dash: Space / Shift / DASH   Pause: P / Esc   Mute: M
