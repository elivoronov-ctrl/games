# Orbit Menders

Pilot Mender-7, a tiny repair pod over a glowing Earth: chase broken satellites, match speed, dock in their rings and steady the wobble to fix them before the sky fills with junk.

**Play:** https://games.getsimpleinvoice.app/orbit-menders/

## Story (6 chapters + epilogue)
Each chapter opens with a cutscene: radio dialogue with Tether Control over a slow cinematic camera orbit.
1. **First Shift**: Juno Halley (Tether Control) and veteran mender Tobs Wren talk you through fixing the weather sats NIMBUS-2 and NIMBUS-3.
2. **Solar Storm**: re-align the folded wings of three HELIOS power platforms while radiation flares bloom as orange shells. Get out before they pop.
3. **The Silent Station**: on Earth's night side, with only city lights below and your headlamp, you wake up Dr. Mirela Vass's three OCULUS telescopes. Dark debris drifts past and the proximity alarm is your friend.
4. **Junk Rain**: a broken rocket stage sends a fast stream of debris through the lane while you fix four satellites.
5. **Lost Signal**: Ana on Tavu Island has lost her school link. Chase the tumbling, drifting LARK-9 comms sat, match its velocity and latch on. Two relay buoys need fixing too.
6. **Cascade**: a Kessler cascade is underway. Debris multiplies every second and a 4-minute countdown is running. Repair five shield-net nodes. Each repaired node shreds the debris around it, and when all five are up the net goes live.
7. **Epilogue: Same Time Tomorrow**.

**Scoring:** 1000 per repair, 150 per spare part, 50 per fuel cell, plus a time bonus against each chapter's par and a hull bonus. If hull or fuel hits zero, you retry the chapter. The high score and the furthest chapter reached are saved locally, so you can continue from that chapter.

## How to repair
Follow the orange beacon or edge arrow. Get your relative speed (Δv) under 6 and glide into the docking ring. Hold Repair: a torque needle wobbles, and progress only builds while it stays in the green. Counter the wobble with strafe. If it hits the red edge you take a surge of damage.

## Controls
| Action | Keyboard / mouse | Touch |
|---|---|---|
| Thrust / strafe | W S / A D | Left virtual stick |
| Up / down | Q / Z (or R / F) | ▲ / ▼ |
| Look / turn | Arrow keys or mouse drag | Drag the right side |
| Boost (uses more fuel) | Shift | BOOST |
| Repair (hold) | Space or E | REPAIR |
| Steady the needle | A / D while repairing | Stick left/right |
| Undock | W / S | Stick up/down |
| Advance / skip story | Space, Enter, click / Esc | Tap the dialogue box / Skip |
| Pause / mute | P or Esc / M | ⏸ / ♪ |

Single player. Three.js (WebGL) + Web Audio, with everything procedural: a shader Earth with clouds, city lights and atmosphere glow, a starfield, lens-flare ghosts, particle thrusters and sparks.
