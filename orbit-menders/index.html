ORBIT MENDERS

You fly Mender-7, a one-seat repair pod on the Halley tether. Six shifts. One crowded sky.

Fly: W/S thrust, A/D strafe, Q/Z (or R/F) up/down, Shift boost. Look with arrow keys or mouse drag.
Dock: follow the orange beacon, slow down (Δv under 6) and glide into the ring.
Repair: hold Space or E. Use A/D to keep the wobble needle in the green.
Survive: grab parts (score + hull) and fuel cells. Dodge debris and flares. If hull or fuel runs out, you retry the chapter.
Touch: left stick thrusts, drag the right side to look, then BOOST, REPAIR and ▲▼.
