A little paper fox must sneak into the Scissor King's workshop and bring back the Great Crease, the folded rainbow that gives the valley its colors.

Move: WASD / arrow keys (touch: joystick · mouse: hold the button to walk toward the pointer).
Fold flat: hold Shift or FOLD. You move slower, sentries see you from half as far, ink stays silent, wind can't push you, and in paper grass you vanish completely.
Toss a paper ball: Space/E or TOSS throws ahead (or right-click a spot). The noise lures sentries away.
Stay out of the sight cones: if a sentry's ? turns into ! and the meter fills, you're snipped back to the last paper crane.
Grab the red wax seal to unlock each gate. Collect the 3 colour stars for points.
