BONEBRUSH

A paleontology mystery in six dig sites.

Uncover every fossil bone hidden in the dig grid before your stamina ⚡ runs out.
🖌 Brush removes one soil layer (1 ⚡). Safe for bones.
⛏ Pick clears a whole tile at once (2 ⚡), but cracks any bone underneath.
📡 Scan shows how many bones hide in a 3×3 area. Limited charges.
Dug-out empty tiles show a number: how many bone tiles touch it (including diagonals). Fossils are connected shapes, so follow the bones. Darker soil means more layers. Amber gives back stamina.
Keys: Arrows/WASD move · Space/Enter dig · 1/2/3 or Q/E tools · P/Esc pause · M mute.
Mouse/touch: pick a tool, tap a tile.
