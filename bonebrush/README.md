# Bonebrush

A paleontology mystery puzzle: brush, pick and scan your way through layered dig sites to uncover fossils without cracking them.

**Play:** https://games.getsimpleinvoice.app/bonebrush/

## Story (6 chapters, 12 digs + epilogue)
1. **Badlands Gully**: Dr. Ada Marl and her survey drone Pip find a carved raptor claw.
2. **Amber Coast**: amber nuggets give back stamina; diver Juno joins the team.
3. **Frozen Cliffs**: ice tiles are one layer deeper.
4. **Tar Pits**: sticky tar costs double to dig.
5. **Sunken Quarry**: boulders only the pick can break, and the rival collector Baron Vellum digs in the wrong place.
6. **The Dragon Bed**: ice, tar and boulders together over the largest fossil of all.

## How it works
- Every tile has 1 to 4 soil layers (darker = deeper, dots show the count).
- **Brush** removes one layer (1 stamina) and is safe for bones.
- **Pick** clears a tile at once (2 stamina) but cracks any bone under it.
- **Scan** shows how many bones hide in a 3x3 area (limited charges).
- Dug-out empty tiles show how many bone tiles touch them. Fossils are connected shapes.
- Finish a dig before stamina runs out. You earn up to 3 stars per dig (complete, no cracks, stamina to spare).

## Controls
| Action | Keyboard | Mouse / Touch |
|---|---|---|
| Move cursor | Arrows / WASD | Hover |
| Dig with current tool | Space / Enter | Click / tap a tile |
| Select tool | 1 / 2 / 3, Q / E (B = brush, X = pick) | Tool buttons |
| Pause / Mute | P or Esc / M | ⏸ / ♪ buttons |

Single player. Canvas 2D + Web Audio, all procedural. Chapter progress, stars and best score are saved locally.
