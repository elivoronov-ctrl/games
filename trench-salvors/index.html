Pilot a tiny salvage sub down the Maw Trench, haul up lost treasure, and upgrade until you can reach the Leviathan's Crown at the very bottom.

Steer: WASD / Arrows or the on-screen stick   Harpoon: Space / HARPOON (stuns creatures)   Sonar ping: Q / PING
Salvage: touch glowing loot to stow it. Surface at the boat to sell, then spend cash at the Shipyard (U) on hull, oxygen, engine, cargo, lamp and harpoon.
Danger: watch O₂ and hull. Below your hull's depth rating the pressure crushes you. Lose all 3 subs and the company folds.
Online: other salvors dive the same trench live. Wave with a sonar ping!
