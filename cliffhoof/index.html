CLIFFHOOF

Bramble the goat kid found the herd's lost bell. Carry it up the jagged peak and ring it at the summit!

Move: W/S or ↑/↓ to walk, A/D or ←/→ to turn (touch: the joystick).
Leap: hold Space (or JUMP, or the mouse button) to charge, release to jump. The dotted arc shows where you'll land: green ring means a safe landing.
Hoof kick: press jump again in mid-air next to a cliff face to kick off it and scramble up.
Pick up alpine flowers for points and touch cairns to set checkpoints. Falling costs a life. Reach the summit flag on each of the 10 climbs.
