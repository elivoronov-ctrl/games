HIVEKEEPERS

Guard the golden hive through six seasons of raiders.

Fly with WASD / arrows, or hold the mouse / drag a finger. Your stinger fires automatically at the nearest raider.
Brush over flowers to gather pollen, then bring it home to the hive: it becomes honey, heals the hive and earns XP.
Each XP level and each cleared day lets you pick 1 of 3 upgrades. Dash (Space / Shift) makes you briefly untouchable.
Survive 6 days: Meadow, Clover Rain, Moth Dusk, Orchard Gale, Beetle Summer and the Hornet Queen.
Online: other keepers buzz through your meadow and share the leaderboard.
