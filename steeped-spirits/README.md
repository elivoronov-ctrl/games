# Steeped Spirits

A teahouse puzzle story: push steaming tea bowls onto glowing mats so every spirit guest crossing the river gets their cup.

**Play:** https://games.getsimpleinvoice.app/steeped-spirits/

2D (Canvas) · single player · story chapters · procedural graphics and Web Audio synth sound (koto-style plucks).

## Story
Grandmother Ume has gone to sleep for a hundred years and left Hana the Last Lantern Teahouse. Every night spirits come for tea, and every bowl has to reach its mat. Bowls can only be pushed, never pulled.

| Chapter | Guest | New mechanic |
|---|---|---|
| 1. First Steam | Kitsune Mori, the fox | Push bowls onto mats |
| 2. Two Kinds of Leaf | Old Kasa, the umbrella spirit | Green matcha and plum hojicha go on matching mats |
| 3. The Polished Floor | Moon Rabbit | Polished boards: bowls slide until they hit something |
| 4. Paper Walls | Kappa of the Ford | Pressure stones open shoji screens |
| 5. Between Rooms | Grandfather Kettle | Spirit doors teleport Hana between rooms |
| 6. The Grey Fog | The Grey Fog | Darkness: you only see by your lantern, with every mechanic combined |
| Epilogue | Every Lantern Lit | |

12 puzzles in total, each one checked solvable by a solver, with the optimal move count used as par. Match par for ★★★. Take too many moves and the tea goes cold (game over for that puzzle). Undo is free and unlimited.

## Controls
| Action | Keyboard | Mouse / touch |
|---|---|---|
| Move / push | Arrows / WASD | Swipe · tap a tile to walk there · tap a neighbouring bowl to push it · on-screen d-pad |
| Undo | Z / U / Backspace | ↶ Undo button |
| Reset puzzle | R | ↻ Reset button |
| Pause / mute | P or Esc / M | ⏸ ♪ buttons |

Single player. Progress, stars and the best score are saved in localStorage. A puzzle-select screen lets you replay any unlocked puzzle.
